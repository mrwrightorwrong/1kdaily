# 1kDaily — COMPLETE PRODUCT MASTER SPECIFICATION

## Save Daily. Build Capacity. Unlock Opportunity.

You are acting as a senior product architect, fintech engineer, UX/UI designer, full-stack developer, database architect, security engineer, QA engineer and product strategist.

Your task is to architect and develop **1kDaily**, a modern, scalable, security-conscious daily savings and credit eligibility platform designed initially for the Nigerian market.

This must not be a basic savings application.

1kDaily is a financial discipline, savings accumulation and structured credit eligibility platform that encourages users to save consistently over a selected period, develop a verifiable financial track record and potentially qualify for loans based on their savings history, financial capacity, risk assessment, repayment affordability and applicable collateral requirements.

The product must be beautiful, intuitive, technologically sound, secure, responsive, financially auditable and commercially scalable.

---

# 1. PRODUCT IDENTITY

## Product name

1kDaily

## Primary tagline

Save Daily. Build Capacity. Unlock Opportunity.

## Brand positioning

1kDaily helps people build financial discipline through daily savings while creating a structured pathway to potential credit opportunities.

The platform encourages consistency, financial responsibility, measurable progress and informed access to credit.

## Target market

Initially target Nigeria, including:

- Salaried workers.
- Small business owners.
- Traders.
- Artisans.
- Freelancers.
- Self-employed individuals.
- Young professionals.
- Students and young adults, where eligible under the applicable legal and product requirements.
- Individuals seeking financial discipline.
- People who may have limited access to conventional credit.

Design the platform in a manner that allows future expansion to other African markets, subject to the appropriate regulatory, financial and operational requirements.

## Product philosophy

1kDaily should be built around five principles:

1. Financial discipline.
2. Transparency.
3. Trust.
4. Financial responsibility.
5. Opportunity.

Never encourage users to take loans they cannot repay.

Never create misleading promises about guaranteed loans, guaranteed returns, guaranteed savings multipliers or guaranteed approval.

# 10. LOAN ELIGIBILITY ENGINE

This is a core differentiating feature of 1kDaily.

The system should use savings behaviour as one component of loan eligibility.

The concept of 2x and 3x savings should be implemented as configurable potential credit ceilings, not automatic entitlements.

## A. MULTIPLIER MODEL

Initial illustrative multiplier levels:

### 2x Potential Credit Ceiling

A potential ceiling of up to twice eligible savings.

### 3x Potential Credit Ceiling

A potential ceiling of up to three times eligible savings.

Administrators must be able to configure:

- Multiplier levels.
- Eligibility criteria.
- Minimum savings duration.
- Minimum savings balance.
- Minimum contribution consistency.
- Previous loan repayment requirements.
- Maximum loan limits.
- Applicable risk restrictions.

Do not automatically grant loans based on the multiplier.

## B. ELIGIBILITY FACTORS

The engine should consider, subject to lawful data processing:

- Verified eligible savings balance.
- Completed savings plans.
- Contribution consistency.
- Missed contribution history.
- Savings duration.
- Previous loan repayment performance.
- Verified income.
- Affordability.
- Existing financial obligations.
- Relevant credit information obtained through lawful and authorised means.
- Guarantors, where applicable.
- Collateral, where applicable.
- Loan amount requested.
- Loan duration.
- Available lending capital.
- Risk policies.
- Applicable regulatory restrictions.

## C. ELIGIBILITY RESULTS

Display an understandable eligibility summary.

Example:

Eligible savings balance: ₦100,000.

Potential 2x ceiling: ₦200,000.

Potential 3x ceiling: ₦300,000.

Actual approved amount: Subject to assessment.

Display the reasons that affect eligibility where reasonably possible.

Do not falsely guarantee credit.

## D. LOAN CAPACITY ENGINE

Implement a modular loan capacity engine.

The engine should:

1. Validate the user's savings history.
2. Determine eligible savings.
3. Calculate preliminary multiplier ceilings.
4. Evaluate repayment capacity.
5. Consider verified income.
6. Consider existing obligations.
7. Consider applicable risk restrictions.
8. Evaluate guarantor or collateral requirements.
9. Apply lending policy.
10. Determine the maximum potentially affordable loan amount.
11. Generate an assessment record.
12. Support manual review where required.

The assessment must be auditable.

Do not use arbitrary hidden rules that cannot be explained or reviewed.

Do not use discriminatory factors or unlawful profiling.

## E. LOAN ELIGIBILITY DASHBOARD

Display:

- Savings balance.
- Eligible savings.
- Savings duration.
- Savings consistency.
- Completed plans.
- Potential 2x limit.
- Potential 3x limit.
- Estimated eligible credit range where appropriate.

- Loan history.
- Repayment history.

- Idempotency keys.
